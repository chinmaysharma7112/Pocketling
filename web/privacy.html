Pnevy privacy

Pnevy is a fairy you can turn on so she flies over your phone screen. There is no account and no ads. Last updated 23 November 2026.

What stays on the phone

  • The fairy color you pick. It is saved on the phone so the same color is used the next time you open Pnevy.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes that saved color.

Drawing over other apps

If you turn the fairy on, Android asks you to allow drawing over other apps. Pnevy uses that only to show her. The window does not take your touches, does not read what is on the screen, and does not see what you type.

Notification

While she is flying, Android keeps a quiet notification so that drawing can stay on. It does not read your other notifications.

Network

Pnevy can use the internet to download a small update of her pages, such as a bug fix. A big change, such as payment, still comes as an update from Google Play. The color you pick is not sent. If the phone is offline, she uses the copy already in the app. The window that flies over other apps does not use the internet.

Microphone, steps, and location

Pnevy does not use the microphone, step counter, or location.

Children

Pnevy does not ask who you are and is not built to collect information about children.

Contact

Use the email shown on the Pnevy page in Google Play.
